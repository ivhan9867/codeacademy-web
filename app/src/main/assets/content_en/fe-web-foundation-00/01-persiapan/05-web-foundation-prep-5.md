Each class has its own discussion forum, accessible from the class detail page. Think of this as a virtual classroom where fellow students help each other outside of your independent study hours.

## What you can do in the forum

- Write questions or share insights related to the class material (maximum 500 characters per post)
- Like (❤) posts that you find helpful so other students know which answers are the most useful
- Refresh the forum to see the latest discussions
- Load older discussions using the "Load older discussions..." button at the bottom

## Brief etiquette

Be polite, stay relevant to the course material, and avoid spam. The forum is active as long as you are logged into the app.

> Before asking, try scrolling through the feed first. Your question might have already been answered by another student in the same class, allowing you to get the answer much faster than waiting for a new reply.
