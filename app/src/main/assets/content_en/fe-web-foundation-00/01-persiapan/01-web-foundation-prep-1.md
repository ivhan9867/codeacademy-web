Before starting, here are a few short ground rules. Think of these as the rules before stepping into the arena — once explained, all that's left is to play.

## Free to use, please do not redistribute

The materials in this course were created specifically to be studied inside this app. You are free to re-read them anytime, test and modify all code examples in the Playground as much as you like, and repeat any module without limits — there is no cap on reading. The only thing to avoid is copying and redistributing this material text elsewhere without permission.

## These materials will continue to grow

This course content may be revised or expanded over time to improve quality and accuracy. If you ever find a section that feels unclear or contains outdated info, that's normal — think of it as using a version that is continuously being refined, not a frozen final product.

## Learn comfortably, not tensely

There are no grades at stake here other than your own progress. Making mistakes when trying code in the Playground is normal and even encouraged — that's how you learn why something can fail. Treat this class as a place to experiment, not an exam with a proctor standing behind you.
