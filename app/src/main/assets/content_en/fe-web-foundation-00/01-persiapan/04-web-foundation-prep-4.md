Learning here is designed like playing a casual game, not like a college lecture with attendance and heavy assignments.

## Step-by-step modules

Each course is broken down into small modules that can be completed little by little — 5 minutes during a class break is enough for one module, so you don't need to wait for hours of free time.

## XP, levels, and streaks

- **XP & Levels**: every completed module adds XP and can raise your level. This tracks your learning progress over time, like an experience bar in a game.
- **Daily streak**: the more consistently you learn every day, the longer your streak grows. Once broken, the count starts over from the beginning — so it's better to study 10 minutes every day than 2 hours once a week.

## Quizzes and certificates

Quizzes at the end of a course exist to make sure the material truly sticks, rather than just being skimmed and forgotten. Once all modules are completed and the quiz is passed, your completion certificate becomes automatically available in the Progress section, without needing to submit a request to anyone.

## No manually graded assignments

All of your practice shows its results directly in the Playground. There is no submission system waiting for someone else to grade, so don't be afraid to make mistakes — experimenting is part of how you learn here, not something that will lower your score.
