This class is the initial gateway to the Front-End journey: covering web concepts, complete HTML, up to basic CSS and positioning.

## Why start here instead of jumping straight into HTML?

Imagine learning HTML and CSS without first knowing what a server or a browser is, or why a page appears on your screen. It might work, but it feels like memorizing a recipe without knowing why water needs to be heated before adding spices — you memorize the tags, but the logic is missing.

Before writing your first line of code, it is important to know: what a website is, why it appears on your mobile screen, and how a request from your phone reaches a server and comes back as the visual display you see.

A website isn't just a place to read news. Money transfers, online photo editing, and watching videos all run through the exact same basic mechanism, and that is what you will learn in this class. Once you understand the foundation, the HTML and CSS modules after this will make much more sense, rather than just memorizing tags one by one.

## What you will get

After completing this class, you will be able to explain how the internet connects computers worldwide, the difference between a website and a web page, the roles of servers and browsers, and how HTML, CSS, and JavaScript work together to form a single page that you see every day.
